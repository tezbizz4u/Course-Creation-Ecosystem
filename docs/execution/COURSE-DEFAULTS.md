## Presentation acceptance criteria

- Relevant to the stated objective and Indian AYUSH practitioner audience; professionally appropriate explanations, examples and teaching depth.
- Clinical claims linked to identifiable reliable sources with publication/version dates; uncertainty and conflicting guidance remain visible. Do not invent references or present generated content as clinically validated.
- Clear instructional sequence, legible hierarchy, consistent typography/colors/spacing, appropriate contrast and purposeful visual communication.
- Original topic-specific diagrams and visuals; avoid decorative stock imagery that does not explain the learning objective.
- Native editable text, shapes, tables and chart elements where the output format supports them. A slide-sized screenshot does not qualify as an editable presentation. Retain image originals and provenance for genuinely raster content.
- No clipped or overflowing text, broken labels, missing assets, unintended overlaps or unreadable references. Check slides at presentation size and in exported output.
- Saved project reopens correctly. PPTX opens in independent viewers with content, geometry, ordering and editing preserved; report any unsupported feature explicitly.
- A conversational request can change content or design and regenerate the affected slides while preserving unrelated edits and version history.
